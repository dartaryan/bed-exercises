1

You move her

She relaxes. Hold the arm or leg above and below the joint and move it slowly. Up to 10 times each side. Stop when you feel resistance.

When she does not want to

  • Accept "no". Try again in about an hour, in a different way.
  • Give her a choice: "Hands or legs?"
  • Put on music she likes. Praise every try.
  • A small goal is fine: "3 ankle pumps."
  • Do small movements during care you already do (washing, turning).

Stop, tell the nurse, then the family

  • Pain, or she grimaces
  • Short of breath
  • Dizzy, pale or sweating
  • Chest pain
  • A tube or bag pulls or leaks
  • The arm with the line hurts or swells
  • A dressing is wet or loose
  • One leg suddenly swollen, red or warm
  • She says stop
